Merge Images into PDF Instantly with In-Browser Security

Converting image collections into standardized, readable PDF files is an everyday requirement for students, accountants, legal practitioners, and digital professionals. Whether compiling scanned bank receipts, photo IDs, portfolio samples, or handwritten class notes, organizing them into a unified PDF format ensures consistent presentation and universal compatibility across all devices. TechLagbe delivers a rapid, clean, and reliable utility that compiles any collection of JPG, PNG, and modern WebP images into a single structured PDF document.

Customizable Layouts: Formats, Margins, and Auto-Orientation

Different documents demand different geometric layouts. Our client-side PDF engine allows granular control over paper sizing, offering standard European ISO A4 formats, American US Letter sizing, and dynamic "Fit-to-Image" scaling for artistic portfolios. Furthermore, you can choose between portrait layouts, landscape spreads, or smart auto-orientation that automatically detects each photo’s aspect ratio and adjusts each page dynamically. Choose zero-margin full-bleed rendering for borderless photography or clean margins for clean, formal office submissions.

Why Client-Side PDF Generation is the Safest Option

Most online image-to-PDF tools force you to upload your sensitive records—such as passports, driving licenses, property deeds, and confidential medical bills—to remote cloud servers. Even when sites claim files are deleted after an hour, your files risk being intercepted or cached in server logs. TechLagbe eliminates this security risk entirely. Leveraging client-side JavaScript execution via the jsPDF engine, all image decoding, scaling, page creation, and binary encoding happen exclusively in your browser’s local memory. No data is transmitted across the internet, ensuring 100% confidentiality.
